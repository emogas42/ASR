The primary objective of the monitoring was to measure progress in dairy, aquaculture, and pomegranate farms in the central, southern, and northern regions, and to assess the effectiveness of the ACT project.

This activity created a strategic reporting base that allowed for transparent measurement of the ACT project's real economic impact and effectiveness on regional businesses, particularly in terms of increasing production volume, growing sales turnover, and multiplying net profit.

ASR Consulting and Research Group conducted an important research project within the framework of a program financed by JICA (Japan International Cooperation Agency) and Tokyo Engineering Consultants Co. (TEC) Ltd. The research was conducted within the "State Program on Provincial Urban Water Supply and Sanitation Project."

The main objective of the survey was to determine the current market prices and productivity of land plots to be used for the construction of water and sewage pipelines. Within the project framework, detailed data on land quality, prices, and perennial plants and trees located on these areas in over 10 villages across 6 different districts of Azerbaijan were collected and a comprehensive report was prepared. For this purpose, our research team held numerous formal and practical meetings with various local government bodies, municipalities, state notaries, and land-owning farmers.

Performance and Impact: The primary strategic benefit of these activities and large-scale field research was to ensure transparency and fairness during infrastructure construction.

The precise data collected formed an invaluable legal-economic basis for the fully correct, fair, and market-value-based calculation of compensation amounts to be paid by the Azerbaijani government to local landowners and farmers whose properties were used for laying water and sewage lines.

Value 15 328 USD
Sector Social impact & evaluation
Location 6 districts (10+ villages)
Donor JICA & Tokyo Engineering
Replacement Costs Infrastructure Research Fair Compensation
