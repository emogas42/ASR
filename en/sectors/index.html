Sectors of activity

We provide a broad range of professional consulting services across economic policy areas and key economic sectors.

Economic policy areas
Agricultural policy We provide expert support in developing agricultural policy, analyzing subsidy and concession mechanisms, and elaborating strategic roadmaps for agro-sector development.
General business environment We provide consulting services on improving the business environment, analyzing investment legislation, and drafting regulatory acts to facilitate entrepreneurial activity.
Competition We offer comprehensive consulting services on competition law for market participants, including assessment of compliance with competition legislation and analysis of antimonopoly policy.
Consumer protection We provide expert support in analyzing consumer rights protection mechanisms, improving complaint systems, and implementing consumer protection legislation.
Industrial zones and industrial policy We offer strategic planning and legal support services for establishing industrial zones, managing industrial parks, and improving the effectiveness of industrial policy.
Market oversight We conduct methodological support and training on improving market oversight mechanisms, analyzing price regulation policy, and ensuring market stability.
Intellectual property We provide legal advisory services for the protection of intellectual property rights, patent and trademark registration, and the commercialization of innovations.
Technical regulation and standardization We provide professional support in implementing technical regulation legislation, assessing compliance with international standards, and organizing certification processes.
SME development We provide strategic advisory services on the development of small and medium-sized enterprises, analysis of state support programs for SMEs, and the establishment of business incubators.
Food safety We provide expert services in applying food safety standards, verifying compliance with AQTA requirements, and managing risks at all stages of the food chain.
Public procurement We provide methodological and legal support for applying public procurement legislation, organizing tender procedures, and increasing the transparency of procurement processes.
