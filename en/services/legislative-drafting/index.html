Work Methodology

Legislative drafting in
6 Steps

From problem diagnostics to parliamentary discussion — a structured and transparent process.

1
Diagnostics
Analysis of the Current State
Systematic analysis of the existing legislative framework, sectoral challenges, gaps, and international best practice in the target area. Stakeholder interviews, focus groups, and data collection.
2
Concept
Drafting Legislative Acts and Amendments
Drafting new law and regulation proposals based on leading international practice, as well as amendment packages to fully align the existing legal framework.
3
Preparation
Writing the Draft Legislation
Based on selected scenarios, preparation of the draft law, explanatory note, and supplementary documents with the involvement of sectoral experts, lawyers, and linguists. Full compliance with international standards.
4
Analysis
Regulatory Impact Assessment
Measurement of the potential effects of the prepared draft law on the socio-economic sphere, business environment, state budget, and investment climate using RIA methodology, and preparation of the assessment report.
5
Consultation
Public Consultation and Coordination
Round tables and public discussions with stakeholders, professional associations, and business associations; refinement of the draft law based on feedback received.
6
Finalisation
Final Package and Parliamentary Support
Delivery of the complete document package (draft law, explanatory note, RIA report, comparative table); provision of technical support to the institution for preparation of parliamentary discussions.
