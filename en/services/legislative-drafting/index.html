Work Methodology

6-Step
Legislative Drafting Process

From problem diagnostics to parliamentary discussions — a structured and transparent process.

1
Diagnostics
Analysis of the Current Situation
Systematic analysis of the existing legislative framework, sectoral problems, gaps, and international best practices in the target area. Interviews with stakeholders, focus groups, and data collection.
2
Concept
Drafting of Legislative Acts and Amendments
Preparation of new draft laws and regulations, as well as amendment packages for full alignment of the existing legal framework, based on advanced international experience.
3
Drafting
Drafting the Law
Preparation of the draft law, explanatory note, and additional documents based on selected scenarios, involving sector specialists, lawyers, and linguists. Full compliance with international standards.
4
Analysis
Regulatory Impact Assessment
Measurement of the developed draft law's potential impacts on the socio-economic sphere, business environment, state budget, and investment climate using RIA methodology, and preparation of the report.
5
Consultation
Public Discussion and Alignment
Roundtables and public discussions with stakeholders, professional associations, and business associations; refinement of the draft law based on feedback received.
6
Completion
Final Package and Parliamentary Support
Delivery of the complete document package (draft law, explanatory note, RIA report, comparative table); provision of technical support to prepare the institution for parliamentary discussions.
