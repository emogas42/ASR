Service 01

Compliance Audit

Identifying deficiencies with gap analysis — 1-5 business days

Sanitary-technical condition, product labeling, supply chain, and documentation audits. A personalized roadmap is prepared to identify and eliminate deficiencies through gap analysis.

Duration1–5 business days
FormatOn-site + remote
ResultGap analysis report
AreaProduction + trade
What's Included
01
Sanitary-technical condition assessment

The current state of production premises, equipment, water supply systems, and sanitation is assessed and compared against regulatory standards.

02
Label and documentation audit

Product labels, quality certificates, technological documents, and internal records are checked; deficiencies are identified.

03
Supply chain inspection

Compliance is checked across the entire chain from raw material suppliers to finished product; critical points are identified.

04
Gap analysis report and roadmap

All findings are compiled into a priority-ranked report; concrete corrective steps are proposed for each gap.

Service 02

HACCP System Implementation

A fully documented, operational HACCP system — 1-3 months

Flow diagrams, hazard analysis, identification of critical control points (CCPs) and critical limits, monitoring system, and full documentation — an operational HACCP system from scratch.

Duration1–3 months
FormatOn-site
ResultHACCP plan
StandardCodex Alimentarius
Implementation Phases
01
Flow diagram and process map

Each stage of the production process is documented; a flow diagram from raw materials to finished product is drawn up and verified on-site.

02
Hazard analysis and CCP identification

Biological, chemical, and physical hazards are identified; critical limits for each CCP are scientifically established.

03
Monitoring system and corrective actions

Monitoring procedures, responsible personnel, and corrective action plans for deviations are prepared for each CCP.

04
Full HACCP documentation

The HACCP plan, work instructions, registration forms, and verification procedures are delivered as a complete document package.

Service 03

ISO 22000 Requirements Implementation

Full system setup with certification support — 3-5 days

Setting up a food safety management system compliant with international standards, with certification support. A personalized approach to implementing ISO 22000:2018 requirements.

Duration3–5 days
StandardISO 22000:2018
ResultCertification readiness
SupportAudit accompaniment
Implementation Phases
01
Initial assessment of existing system

A comparative analysis against ISO 22000:2018 requirements is conducted; existing elements are identified and those that need to be established are determined.

02
Preparation of FSMS documents

Policies, objectives, procedures, work instructions, and registration forms are prepared in accordance with ISO 22000 requirements.

03
Preparation for certification audit

An internal audit is conducted, existing nonconformities are eliminated; full readiness for the certification body audit is ensured.

Service 04

Traceability System

Full traceability system compliant with NK 107 requirements — 2-4 weeks

Establishment of lot numbering, recall plan, and registration system based on the "one step back – one step forward" principle in accordance with NK 107 resolution.

Duration2–4 weeks
BasisNK 107 resolution
ResultRecall plan
SystemLot numbering
Implementation Phases
01
Establishment of lot numbering system

A unique lot number format is determined for each batch; production date, shift, and raw material lot data are linked to the lot.

02
Registration forms and procedures

Forms, a journal system, and electronic tracking procedures are prepared to record all movements from raw material receipt to dispatch.

03
Recall plan

A recall procedure for potential problem cases, responsible persons, contact lists, and a simulation test plan are prepared.

FormatOn-site + Project
ResultTechnological plan

03
