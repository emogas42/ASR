Methodology

Our Strategic Management
Process

We deliver strategic planning services through a results-oriented, step-by-step methodology aligned with international standards.

01
Diagnostics and Data Collection
A comprehensive diagnostic of the institution's current state is conducted through interviews, surveys, and analysis of existing documents. Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats are identified.
02
Strategic Analysis and Prioritization
Collected data is systematically analyzed, strategic directions are identified, and priority program and project activities are ranked.
03
Development of the Strategic Plan
A complete strategic plan document is prepared in accordance with international methodologies: including mission, vision, strategic objectives, specific tasks, implementers, timeline, and indicators.
04
Stakeholder Alignment
The developed strategic plan is discussed with leadership, staff, and key stakeholders, feedback is collected, and the document is refined. The final version is formulated based on consensus.
05
Implementation and Support
During the implementation phase of the strategic plan, support is provided for staff capacity building, process redesign, and budget synchronization.
06
Monitoring and Review
External monitoring and evaluation of strategic plan implementation is conducted, reports are prepared, and strategic directions are reviewed as needed.
