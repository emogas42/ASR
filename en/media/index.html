ASR Group Launches Strategic Partnership with Uzbekistan Food Industry Association
8 June 2026

An official business meeting was held in Tashkent between ASR Group leadership and the Uzbekistan Food Industry Association.

More details

An official business meeting was held in Tashkent between "ASR Distribution and Development Group" and the leadership of the Uzbekistan Food Industry Association. Company founder Elxan Mikayilov and CEO Ali Valiyev personally attended the meeting, where discussions covered food safety reforms, the transition to a risk-based inspection system, and international standards including ISO 22000 and HACCP. The meeting concluded with an agreement to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) in the coming days.

  • Risk-based inspection transition, ISO 22000 and HACCP standards discussed

AQTA called on small and medium-sized businesses to implement HACCP
23 December 2025

Small and medium-sized business entities must implement the HACCP system in their food facilities by June 30, 2026.

More details

AQTA has addressed small and medium-sized business entities. It is mandatory to implement the Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP) system in food facilities by June 30, 2026. This requirement aims to maintain food safety standards and ensure compliance with international requirements.

  • Deadline: 30 June 2026
  • HACCP system implementation is mandatory
  • Applies to small and medium-sized business entities
  • Compliance with international standards will be ensured

Frequently asked questions

What services does ASR Group provide?
ASR Development Consulting Group is a leading consulting company operating since 2009. It provides comprehensive services in: strategic management and monitoring systems; food safety and quality standards (HACCP, ISO 22000, BRCGS, IFS); legislative and legal expertise; technical-economic research and analytical studies; agricultural strategies and innovations; healthcare sector consulting and pharma; international cooperation and project management; as well as corporate training and professional development programs.
Do you have international certifications?
Yes, our company holds international accreditations. We provide certification and audit services in food safety under HACCP, ISO 22000, BRCGS, and IFS standards. We ensure compliance with ISO 14001 for environmental management and ISO 9001 for quality management. These certifications are recognized by the European Union, Codex Alimentarius, and international organizations, and are fully compliant with Azerbaijani legislation.
In which sectors do you operate?
We operate in: public administration and strategic planning; food safety and standardization; healthcare sector and pharma; agriculture and agrarian innovations; economic policy and legislation; energy and environment; as well as international cooperation and investment projects. With our specialized expert teams tailored to each sector and 16 years of experience, we serve both public and private companies.
