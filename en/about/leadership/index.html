About

Dr. Elxan Mikayilov (Ph.D. in Economics) is a leading management and consulting expert with over 18 years of deep institutional experience in public administration, strategic planning, food safety, and business development. Throughout his career, he has served as Sector Head for Economic Reforms and Agricultural Issues at the Administration of the President of Azerbaijan, as well as Chief of Staff and Advisor to the Chairman at the Food Safety Agency (AQTA). He played a pivotal role in establishing the country's food safety and sanitary-phytosanitary system, aligning food businesses with HACCP and ISO 22000 standards, and building a special star classification system. In the field of strategic management, he directly led the development of the 1,000-page "Strategic Roadmap" and the "State Programme on Ensuring Food Safety." Leading the Secretariat of the State Commission on Improving the Investment Climate, the reforms he carried out advanced Azerbaijan 25 places in the Doing Business ranking to reach 32nd position. He also participated in establishing SMBA, the Intellectual Property Agency, and the Anti-Monopoly Agency. In the legislative sphere, he contributed to drafting the Laws on Food Safety, Plant Health, Technical Regulation, and Standardization, and officially represented Azerbaijan in international working groups of the WTO and ISO. He currently serves as CEO of ASR Development Consulting Group, providing strategic consulting, management, and international standards compliance services to the public and private sectors.
