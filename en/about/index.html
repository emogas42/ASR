About the company

Strategic solutions, sustainable growth

ASR Development Consulting Group LLC is a leading Azerbaijani consulting firm providing consulting, outsourcing, training, research, and professional advisory services aimed at developing the institutional, legal, and strategic management capacity of the public and private sectors. Founded in April 2009 to support socio-economic reforms in the country and build highly reliable local institutional capacity, the company today operates with a team of 50+ professional local and international consultants with deep expertise in management and technical fields. In 2019, in response to the large-scale structural reforms carried out by the government and the emerging challenges and service demands of the market, the company completely renewed its mission, goals, and service concept. Today, our leadership team consists of strategic specialists who have gained management experience in large-scale programs of global donor organizations such as USAID, the World Bank, the European Union, and SECO. The company has actively participated in developing the Strategic Roadmaps of the national economy, designing state-level institutional reforms (establishment of AQTA, SMBA, Intellectual Property Agency, etc.) and drafting more than 60 regulatory and legislative acts across various policy areas.

MISSION

To provide consulting, outsourcing, training, research, and other professional services aimed at developing institutional and strategic management capacity, improving legislation, and raising the compliance level of the private sector in order to enhance the effectiveness of activities directed at the development of the public and private sectors.

VISION

To become one of the leading and most trusted companies in the consulting market within our segment and to make significant contributions to the socio-economic development of Azerbaijan through our services.

Our history

From rebranding to leadership: The ASR story

Founded in 2009 with the goal of supporting the advancement of economic and social reforms in Azerbaijan, our company has grown over the decades into one of the most trusted consulting groups in the country.

2009

Company founded

ASR Consulting and Research Group LLC was established in April. The company was founded to meet the need for experienced local and international specialists skilled in providing practical training and consulting services in organizational management and socio-economic development to local institutions, and to build highly reliable institutional capacity to support local government and international organizations in advancing economic and social reforms in Azerbaijan.

2009 – 2018

Active operations period

ASR Group continued its active operations throughout this period, delivering services through high-quality research, consulting, and training, while applying the principles of continuous client satisfaction and quality improvement. The company carried out its activities with the mission of enhancing the competitiveness of the Azerbaijani private sector and strengthening the institutional performance of local government bodies.

2019

Restructuring & rebranding

In February, the company was reorganized and restructured with a renewed mission and objectives in response to the significant reforms carried out by the Azerbaijani government in economic and social areas, as well as the emergence of new market challenges and corresponding service demands. The mission, objectives, and services were reviewed in the context of current socio-economic and market trends, updated through a long-term planning process, and the organization was renamed ASR Development Consulting Group LLC.

Present day

Regional leadership position

With its renewed objectives and strong institutional capacity, ASR Development Consulting Group currently maintains its leading position as one of the most trusted consulting groups in Azerbaijan and the region, with a team of 50+ professional local and international consultants and over 100 successfully completed projects.
