Official Presentation of the 'Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club (UZAZ Business Club)' Held in Tashkent

18 July 2026 Tashkent, Uzbekistan Press Release ASR Group

Tashkent, July 18, 2026 – The official presentation ceremony of the 'Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club (UZAZ Business Club)' was successfully held in Tashkent on July 18, 2026, aimed at strengthening economic cooperation between Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan, expanding business ties among entrepreneurs, and promoting new partnerships.

Organising Partners
Networkstan
ASR Distribution and Development Group
Azerbaijan Entrepreneurs Confederation
Uzbekistan Chamber of Commerce and Industry

The presentation ceremony was attended by representatives of government agencies from Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan, entrepreneurs, investors, heads of business associations, members of the diplomatic corps, and public figures. The event was assessed as an important platform for strengthening economic ties between the two countries, increasing mutual investment opportunities, and developing direct cooperation between business communities.

Opening Remarks

Opening the event with welcoming remarks, Elxan Mikayılov, Chairman of the Board of the UzAz Business Club, provided detailed information about the main objectives behind the establishment of the 'Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club (UZAZ Business Club)', the Club's areas of activity, and its plans for the future.

The Club will serve to unite entrepreneurs of Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan on a single platform, fostering the formation of new partnerships and the systematic development of business ties.

— Elxan Mikayılov, Chairman of the Board of the UzAz Business Club
Networkstan Address

Vüqar Mirzəyev, Executive Director of Networkstan, then took the floor to speak about services offered to foreign entrepreneurs wishing to establish businesses in Uzbekistan, business organisation, mechanisms of cooperation with government agencies, and the opportunities the 'Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club (UZAZ Business Club)' will create for entrepreneurs.

Other Speakers

The event also featured addresses by Alisher Shaykhov, representative of the Uzbekistan Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Bekzod Erjigitov, representative of the Ministry of Investment, Industry and Trade of the Republic of Uzbekistan, and Elvin Ali, representative of the Azerbaijan Entrepreneurs Confederation, who shared their views on the development prospects of Azerbaijan–Uzbekistan economic relations, the expansion of cooperation between the private sectors, and the significance of business platforms.

Club Activities

During the event, the activities of the 'Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club (UZAZ Business Club)', membership opportunities, services to be offered to entrepreneurs, and projects planned for the upcoming period were presented. Participants were informed about business forums, investment missions, B2B meetings, dialogue platforms between government agencies and business representatives, and other initiatives the Club plans to organise.

The B2B meetings and networking session held after the official part created opportunities for participants to establish new business contacts, discuss potential partnerships, and identify directions for future cooperation.

The 'Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club (UZAZ Business Club)' sets as its main goal to act as a business bridge between Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan, contributing to the development of sustained cooperation among entrepreneurs, investors and government agencies, promoting mutual investments, and further strengthening the economic relations between the two countries.

The presentation ceremony was highly appreciated by participants and was assessed as one of the important steps taken towards developing Azerbaijan–Uzbekistan business cooperation in the future.
