Meeting with Uzbekistan Chamber of Commerce and Industry: "Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club" to Be Established

9 June 2026 Tashkent, Uzbekistan Business Relations

Tashkent, 09.06.2026 – An important meeting was held with the Uzbekistan Chamber of Commerce and Industry with the aim of expanding ties between Uzbek and Azerbaijani businesspeople, assessing existing opportunities, and elevating relations between entrepreneurs to an institutional level.

The meeting was attended by Elxan Mikayilov, founder of "ASR Distribution and Development Group", CEO Ali Valiyev, the representative of the Azerbaijan Entrepreneurs Confederation in Uzbekistan, and the leadership of the "Networkistan" organisation.

During the discussions, the importance of placing the dialogue between the business communities of the two countries on a more systematic and sustainable footing was emphasised.

To this end, a decision was taken to institutionalise the organisations that established these ties, and it was resolved that, as a first step, the "Uzbekistan-Azerbaijan Business Club" would be formed to ensure the active participation of entrepreneurs from both sides.

This newly established business club will serve as a platform for entrepreneurs to regularly come together, implement joint projects, and strengthen mutually beneficial partnerships.
